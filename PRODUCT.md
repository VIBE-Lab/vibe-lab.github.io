# VIBE Lab Website Product Brief

## Product

VIBE Lab is an academic research group website for Visual Intelligence and Biomedical Exploration at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. The site introduces the group, research directions, publications, team members, news, and released datasets or software.

## Audience

Primary visitors are potential students, academic collaborators, biomedical AI researchers, clinical partners, and users of VIBE Lab datasets or software.

## Brand Register

The website should feel academic, precise, visually intelligent, and practical. It should avoid generic AI landing-page tropes, cluttered inherited lab templates, unlicensed photo-heavy layouts, and decorative elements that distract from research content.

## Visual Direction

Blue is the primary color, red is the secondary accent, and green is the tertiary accent. The visual system should support both light and dark modes, with clear typography, strong information hierarchy, compact navigation, and polished but restrained interface details.

## Accessibility

The site should remain responsive, readable, keyboard navigable, and comfortable in both themes, with meaningful alt text, sufficient contrast, and layouts that do not depend on decorative images.
