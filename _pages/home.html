XJTLU biomedical AI research group

VIBE Lab

Visual Intelligence and Biomedical Exploration

We are a research group focused on visual intelligence and medical image analysis. Our work starts from real biomedical and clinical problems, with the goal of making AI methods that can move from research prototypes into practical, trustworthy use. We also care about the craft of research software: tools should be technically strong, visually clear, and genuinely easy for people to use.
