Research

Visual intelligence for biomedical problems that matter.

VIBE Lab develops AI methods for medical image analysis with a strong focus on practical biomedical use. We work on segmentation, representation learning, computational pathology, multimodal biomedical AI, and usable research software that can help models move from papers into real workflows.

Featured Project

Cross-Organ and Cross-Scanner Adenocarcinoma Segmentation (COSAS 2024)

COSAS 2024 studies adenocarcinoma segmentation under the domain shifts that make computational pathology hard to deploy: different organs, scanners, staining appearances, and acquisition conditions. The challenge is a natural research anchor for VIBE Lab because it connects robust visual intelligence, pathology segmentation, and real-world evaluation.

We use this project to explore methods that generalize beyond a single laboratory setting and to build resources that help the community compare algorithms under clinically meaningful variation.
